🎬 PSO2 Animation Viewer

Preview any in-game animation (Emotes & Motions) in 3D and swap them directly into your game.

Download Program

How to Use

1
Load a test model: In the top panel, click M1 or M2 to load a default 3D mannequin.
2
Link your game data directory: Click pso2 data… and select your game's pso2_bin/data/win32 folder (or win32reboot). This allows the viewer to extract and display weapons, props, and VFX in real time.
3
Search and preview animations: Filter by Emotes or Motions, and search by English name, Japanese name, or chat command (e.g. /sivapose1). If an emote has multiple hand poses or click stages, press the HSI / CLICK button to cycle through variants.
4
Swap animations in-game: Switch to the Replace tab. Under 1) ORIGINAL, select the base animation you own in-game (e.g. Sit 1). Under 2) NEW, choose the animation you want to see. Click Apply to Game. An automatic backup is created in ./portable/backups so you can revert anytime with 1 click.
💡 Built-in Mod Manager: You can install mods from .zip archives in the Mods tab and toggle them on/off. It automatically resolves conflicts by deactivating older mods modifying the same animation.

Live 3D viewport playback and in-game animation replacement demo.

👗 CMX Helper Tool

Enables jackets over outfits that normally don't support them, and toggles hidden ornamentation layers.

Download Program

How to Use

1
Open the tool alongside your game or the Animation Viewer.
2
Enable Outerwear (Jackets): Turn Allow Outerwear: ON to allow equipping outer jackets over base costumes in the Salon that are locked by default.
3
Hide Ornamentation: Turn on Hide Ornament 1 or Hide Ornament 2 to hide specific clothing ornaments or stage parts in the Salon.

1. Enable outer jackets over base costumes in Salon.

2. Toggle and hide clothing ornamentation parts.

📺 PSO2 Video Studio

All-in-one video modding and customization suite for Phantasy Star Online 2 (Classic & NGS). Replace cutscenes, lobby monitors, openings, and create multi-screen composite textures with SEGA CRI USM encryption.

Download Program
⚡ What is this program? PSO2 Video Studio is an all-in-one video modding and customization suite designed for Phantasy Star Online 2 (PSO2 Classic and New Genesis / NGS). The game uses a proprietary format called CRI Sofdec (USM) with SEGA encryption and custom audio tracks (ADX/HCA).
  • View & Play: Play any internal video from the game (openings, cutscenes, concerts, lobby monitors).
  • Drag & Drop Replacement: Replace any video with your own files (.mp4, .avi, .mkv, etc.) with automatic resolution & profile matching.
  • Multi-Screen Collage: Build coordinated multi-screen mosaic textures for lobby and command bridge displays (monitor_commander_001 & monitor_shop_001).
  • Multi-Version Management: Keep multiple versions for each mod, disable/enable, toggle between versions, or restore the original SEGA video with one click.
  • Pack & Share: Package and install ready-to-share .zip mods for the community.
  • Automatic Integrity Bypass (SpecialIce): Automatically ensures modded files load properly in-game without being reverted or ignored by the official launcher.

How to use each tab

1
Tab 1: Video Explorer (Complete Catalog & Instant Modding)
Purpose: A complete catalog containing all 780+ PSO2 and NGS internal video files, organized by internal name, folder, and in-game purpose.
How to use it:
  • Search & Filter: Use the search bar or the Classic / NGS filter buttons to find any video (e.g. ui_title_loop_01.usm for the title screen loop).
  • Preview: Click on any video in the list to watch and listen with synchronized video and audio playback.
  • Replace Video (Instant Modding): Simply drag & drop your video file (.mp4, etc.) directly onto the video player area. The application automatically detects the profile and resolution, encodes & encrypts with SEGA keys, backs up the original video, and injects it into the game with an immediate preview.
  • Extract Video: Click this button to export an original game video to your PC as .mp4 or .usm.

Demo: Replace and install in-game videos instantly via Drag & Drop.

2
Tab 2: Installed Mods (Version Control & Management)
Purpose: Manage all currently modded videos in your client, keeping multiple alternatives and revision history without losing any files.
How to use it:
  • In-Game Version (Version Dropdown): If you replace a video multiple times (e.g. Video A, Video B, Video C), they are saved as Version 1, Version 2, etc. Select which version you want active in-game directly from the dropdown.
  • Disable Mod / Enable Mod: Temporarily disable the mod to play the original SEGA game video without deleting your mods, or re-enable it with a single click.
  • + Save Version: Saves the currently active in-game video as a new version snapshot.
  • Delete Version: Permanently deletes the selected version. If you delete the last remaining mod version, the game automatically restores the original SEGA video.
  • Uninstall Mod: Fully uninstalls the mod, removes all stored versions and backups, and restores the original video to 100%.
  • Export Mod / Export All: Exports one or all active mods into a distribution-ready .zip archive.
  • Bypass (SpecialIce): Displays the checksum bypass status (Bypass: OK). If outdated or missing, click it to install it with one click.
  • Deep Scan: Performs an asynchronous background scan across your game drive to locate any external or manually placed mod backups without freezing the UI.

Demo: Manage versions, toggle mods on/off, and restore original videos.

3
Tab 3: Mod Installer (Install Community ZIP Packs)
Purpose: Easily install mod packs shared by other community creators.
How to use it:
  • Drag and drop any .zip file into the dashed drop area (or click Browse ZIP).
  • The application scans the archive and lists all detected mod files.
  • Check the boxes for the videos you wish to install.
  • Click "Install Mod Package into Game". It will automatically back up your original files, place the modded videos into their exact subdirectories, and guarantee the checksum bypass is enabled.
4
Tab 4: Multi-Screen Studio (Synchronized Monitor Collages)
Purpose: Design composite in-game monitor textures by combining several videos into a single coordinated display matching the game's 3D UV mapping:
  • monitor_shop_001: A 4-screen 2x2 grid layout used for lobby shopping kiosk screens.
  • monitor_commander_001: The giant curved command bridge screen, joining 6 video feeds: 4 horizontal monitors and 2 vertical side monitors (which are automatically rotated 90° to match the texture map layout).

How to use it:
  • Select your target monitor template (monitor_shop_001 or monitor_commander_001).
  • Drag and drop individual videos from Windows Explorer directly onto each slot box (Slot 1, Slot 2, Slot 3, etc.).
  • Configure the output duration (shorter videos loop automatically until reaching the target length) and select which audio feed to retain.
  • Click "Play Realtime Preview" to inspect a live simulation of all videos animating together.
  • Click "Generate & Install Mod into Game" to compile the video into the official PSO2 format and inject it straight into your game.

Demo: Multi-Screen Studio - Merge and synchronize multiple videos into complex monitor textures.
